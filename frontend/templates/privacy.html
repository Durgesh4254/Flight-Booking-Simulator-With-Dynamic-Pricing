Data Protection

FlyEase Privacy Policy

We prioritize transparency and passenger data protection. Read how your booking details and simulator records are handled.

1. Information We Collect

We collect passenger full names, date of birth, contact phone numbers, email addresses, and passport/government ID numbers solely for the purpose of issuing verifiable E-Tickets and seat manifests.

2. How Data is Processed & Secured

All transmission between your browser and our Django backend is encrypted with 256-bit SSL/TLS protocols. Passwords and sensitive authentication tokens are hashed using industry-standard cryptographic algorithms.

3. Simulator Scope & Payment Tokens

FlyEase is an educational aviation dynamic pricing simulator. We never store real credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank account credentials. All transactions use randomized simulated token IDs.

4. User Rights & Data Deletion

You may request the deletion of your account and booking history at any time by contacting our privacy compliance officer at privacy@flyease.aero.
