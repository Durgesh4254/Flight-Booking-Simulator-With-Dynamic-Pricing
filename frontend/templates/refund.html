Cancellation & Refund Policy

Clear, transparent guidelines on booking cancellations, automated refund calculations, and ledger processing.

FULL REFUND (100%)
Cancellations >24 Hours

Cancellations made at least 24 hours prior to scheduled flight departure are eligible for a 100% refund of base fare, dynamic fare, and add-on fees.

STANDARD REFUND (75%)
Cancellations <24 Hours

Cancellations made within 24 hours of departure are refunded at 75% of total ticket value, with 25% retained as airline seat reallocation surcharge.

Instant Refund Processing

All approved refund requests are calculated automatically and reflected instantly in your transaction history ledger and payment receipt records.
