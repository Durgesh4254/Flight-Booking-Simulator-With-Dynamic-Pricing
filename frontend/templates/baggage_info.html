Baggage Allowance & Guidelines

Know your baggage weight limits, dimension rules, and upgrade pricing before arriving at the airport terminal.

Cabin / Hand Baggage

FREE WITH ALL TICKETS
  • Weight Limit: 1 piece up to 7 kg (15 lbs).
  • Dimensions: Maximum 55 cm × 35 cm × 25 cm (including wheels and handles).
  • Personal Item: 1 additional small laptop bag, purse, or infant diaper bag.
  • Liquids: Max 100ml per container in transparent, resealable pouch.

Check-in Baggage

STANDARD INCLUDED
  • Domestic Flights: 15 kg (33 lbs) per passenger.
  • International Flights: 25 kg to 30 kg depending on route and airline.
  • Extra Baggage Upgrade: +15 kg additional luggage available for ₹1,200 during checkout.
  • Overweight Fees: Pre-book online to save up to 40% compared to airport check-in desk rates.
