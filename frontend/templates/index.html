FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a real flight booking platform?

FlyEase is a flight booking simulator built with Django. No real money or tickets are involved. All payments are mocked using a simulated payment gateway.

How does dynamic pricing work?

Our algorithm calculates fares based on a base price, seat availability (load factor), days until departure, and booking time. The fewer seats remain, the higher the fare — just like real airlines.

Can I book a round-trip?

Yes! Select "Round-trip" on the search form, pick your return date, and the system will let you choose departure and return flights separately — each with their own seat selection.

What are the coupon codes for testing?

You can add coupons via the Django admin panel. Create a Coupon object with a code, discount percentage or flat amount, and expiry date.
