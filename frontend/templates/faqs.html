Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to frequently asked questions regarding FlyEase dynamic pricing, ticket verification, and multi-passenger bookings.

1. How does FlyEase Dynamic Pricing calculate ticket fares?

Our pricing algorithm simulates real-world airline revenue management. Ticket prices dynamically adjust based on remaining seat capacity (occupancy %), booking lead time before departure, high-demand peak travel hours, and route popularity factors.

2. How do round-trip bookings and E-Tickets work?

When booking a round-trip, FlyEase handles both outbound and return flight legs within a single checkout. Upon payment confirmation, the platform generates **two distinct, verified PDF E-Tickets** with unique PNRs, departure schedules, and seat allocations.

3. Can I undo or change my selected seats?

Yes! You can click the **Undo** button in the seat map header to remove your most recent selection, click **Reset** to clear all chosen seats, or directly click an active seat on the 3D cabin map to toggle its selection.

4. Are payments real or simulated?

FlyEase operates as an advanced airline simulator. All payment flows (Card, UPI, Net Banking, Wallet, Counter) utilize simulated test tokens with 256-bit validation without charging actual bank accounts.

5. Where can I find my past bookings?

Logged-in users can navigate to My Trips anytime to view active flight radar routes, check status, and re-download verified single-page E-Ticket PDFs.
